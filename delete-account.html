Delete your account and data

This page explains how to delete your Routine Clash account and everything linked to it.

In the app

Open Profile › My account › Delete account and confirm. It is immediate and cannot be undone.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, or cannot sign in, email suport.routineclash@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, and tell us your username. We delete the account and confirm by email within 30 days.

Delete only some data, without deleting the account

In the app you can delete these one by one, without touching anything else:

  • tasks — in the Diary, open the task and delete it;
  • goals and each goal's entries;
  • lists and each list's items;
  • proof photos and your profile photo;
  • comments you wrote;
  • groups you created.

What you delete this way is removed from the server straight away; the account and everything else stay as they are. To delete other data without deleting the account (for example your game progress or messages), email suport.routineclash@gmail.com from your account's email address, tell us your username and what you want deleted. We reply within 30 days.

What is deleted when you delete the account

Everything: your profile, email, date of birth, tasks, goals, lists, completions, XP, photos, messages, friends, the groups you created, your game progress (bixos, chests, island, coins and diamonds), your PvP trophies and fights, and your damage to the group boss. Your username becomes free for anyone to take.

What is kept after deleting the account

Nothing. We keep no copy after you delete your account.
