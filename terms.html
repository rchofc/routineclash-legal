Terms of Use

version: 2 (8 October 2026)

These are the rules of Routine Clash. By creating an account, you accept what is written here.

In case of any discrepancy, the Portuguese version of this document prevails.

What Routine Clash is

An application for organising tasks, habits and goals, and for doing them in company: you can share what you do with friends, challenge them, and join groups. You earn XP and level up as you get things done.

The app is offered as is. We do our best to keep it working well, but we do not promise it will never fail or that it will always be available.

Who can use it

You must be 13 or older. We ask for your date of birth when you create the account.

The account is yours and yours alone. Do not lend it, sell it, or let anyone else use it. You are responsible for everything that happens in it and for keeping your password secret — if you suspect someone got hold of it, change it and sign out of your other devices.

What you put in the app

You write tasks, take proof photos, send messages and create groups. That is yours, and stays yours. We only need to be able to store it and show it to the people you chose to show it to — nothing more. We do not use what you write or photograph for any other purpose.

Remember that a proof photo is seen by other people. Before you take it, look at what is around you.

What you may not do

  • Post illegal, violent, sexual or hateful content, or anything that harasses someone.
  • Photograph or post other people without their agreement.
  • Pretend to be someone else.
  • Bother, stalk or threaten anyone, including through the alarm you can send to a friend.
  • Work around the rules of the game — faking proof, creating accounts to challenge yourself, or exploiting bugs to gain XP.
  • Try to get into accounts that are not yours, or deliberately overload our services.
  • Copy or resell the app.

If you find a bug that grants extra XP, tell us instead of using it. Noticing it is not forbidden; taking advantage of it is.

Reports, and what happens to those who abuse

You can report a post, a message or a person from inside the app. We read the reports.

If someone breaks these rules, we may delete what they posted, remove features from them, or close their account. In a serious case — illegal content, or someone in danger — we will do so without prior notice.

You can also block a person: you stop seeing each other and the friendship is removed.

XP, levels and rewards

XP, levels, streaks and anything else you earn have no monetary value. They cannot be bought, sold or transferred between accounts, and cannot be exchanged for anything outside the app.

The same goes for everything in the game — bixos, chests, items, weapons, the island, coins and diamonds. They are earned by completing tasks and playing, never with money: the app has no purchases. What comes out of a chest is random, but chests are never paid for with real money.

In the group boss and in PvP you play with other people: your name, your trophies and your defending team are visible to whoever plays with you. Using bugs or other accounts to win trophies or prizes counts as getting around the rules of the game.

We may adjust the rules of the game — what each thing is worth, the daily caps — to keep it balanced. We do not take away XP already earned because of a new rule.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time, in Profile. It is immediate and deletes everything. Your username becomes free for someone else.

We may close an account that breaks these rules. If we ever discontinue the app, we will give notice so you can take what is yours.

Liability

Routine Clash is a tool to help you get organised. **It is not medical, psychological or health advice.** The screen-time goals and habits you record are yours, and what to do about them is your decision.

We are not responsible for what other people write or post in the app, nor for data loss caused by things outside our control.

Alarms depend on your phone. **Some manufacturers stop applications from waking themselves up**, and in those cases the alarm may not ring no matter what we do — the app has a screen to help you fix that, but we cannot guarantee it works on every device. Do not use Routine Clash as your only alarm clock for something you really cannot miss.

Changes to these rules

If we change anything important, the app will tell you and ask you to accept again. The version is at the top of this page.
